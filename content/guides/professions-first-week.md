## The rule of thumb: one gathering, one crafting

Level one gathering profession and one crafting profession. Gathering funds the crafting, and the crafting turns cheap materials into things other players want immediately: bags, potions, weapon stones, ammo, enchants.

Two crafting professions on one character is a gold sink you cannot afford in week one.

## Pairing table

| Pairing | Why it works | First-week value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Herbalism + Alchemy | Herbs feed potions; potions sell to every dungeon group. | High — healing and mana potions move fast. |
| Mining + Blacksmithing | Ore feeds weapons and armor; stones sell to melee. | Medium — good if you are the group's plate wearer. |
| Mining + Engineering | Ore feeds gadgets, ammo, and explosives. | Medium — engineering ammo and utility sell well. |
| Skinning + Leatherworking | Skins feed leather armor and bags. | Medium — depends on how many leather wearers are leveling. |
| Tailoring + Enchanting | Cloth feeds bags; disenchanting feeds enchants. | High — bags are the single best early seller. |
| Herbalism + Tailoring | Cloth and herbs have no overlap, but both sell raw. | Low — pure gathering, no crafting payoff. |

**Do not** pair two gathering professions unless you are deliberately playing a bank alt. You will drown in materials and have nothing to sell that is not also being farmed by everyone else.

## What sells first

1. **Bags.** Tailoring bags are the highest-demand item in the first week. Every player needs inventory space, and nobody has enough cloth to make their own.
2. **Potions and elixirs.** Dungeon groups buy healing and mana potions continuously. Alchemy pays for itself fast.
3. **Enchanting materials.** Greens that nobody needs become dust and essences. An enchanter in the group turns trash into gold — or free enchants for the group.
4. **Weapon stones and ammo.** Melee and hunters always need them; blacksmithing and engineering supply them.
5. **Cooking food with buffs.** Group buff food sells once players start running dungeons seriously.

## Secondary skills your group actually needs

- **First Aid — everyone.** Bandages are the only self-sustain for several classes while leveling.
- **Cooking — at least one person in the group.** Food buffs matter once dungeon runs get long.
- **Fishing — optional, but good idle income.** Also the natural partner to cooking.

One Forever-specific detail from beta coverage: **campfires have a short reuse cooldown/debuff**. If your group leans on campfire cooking, rotate who places the fire instead of spamming it.

## Per-role quick plan

- **Tank:** Mining + Blacksmithing if you are plate; otherwise Mining + Engineering for utility.
- **Healer:** Herbalism + Alchemy. Potions are your insurance.
- **Caster DPS:** Tailoring + Enchanting. Cloth is free for you, and disenchanting pays.
- **Physical DPS:** Skinning + Leatherworking, or Mining + Blacksmithing depending on armor type.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Dumping gold into skill 300 on day one.** Level professions as you level your character; the materials come to you.
- **Ignoring dungeon recipes.** Recipe drops from dungeons are often the best thing a crafter can sell in week one.
- **Selling raw materials you will need at 60.** Bank a stack of the tier you will need for your own gear before you sell the rest.
- **Forgetting the auction house cut.** Thin margins on raw materials disappear after fees; crafted goods usually hold value better.
