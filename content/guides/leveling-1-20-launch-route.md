## Before the character screen

Decide three things before you log in, because changing them later costs an evening:

- **Faction and ruleset** with your group — see the [start-here guide](/guides/start-here-rulesets-and-realms).
- **Starting zone** you can tolerate for four hours.
- **Addons.** A quest/map addon is close to mandatory for launch-week density. Set it up before the queue, not after.

## 1–10: where each race starts

| Race | Starting zone | Level 10 handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human | Elwynn Forest | Stormwind |
| Dwarf / Gnome | Dun Morogh | Ironforge → Stormwind |
| Night Elf | Teldrassil | Darnassus → Stormwind |
| Orc / Troll | Durotar | Orgrimmar |
| Tauren | Mulgore | Thunder Bluff → Orgrimmar |
| Undead | Tirisfal Glades | Undercity → Orgrimmar |

Two launch-week realities:

1. **Starting zones are the most crowded maps in the game on day one.** Named mobs and quest items will be contested. Do not expect a clean run.
2. **The 1–10 stretch is short.** If your group scatters now, regrouping at 10 costs less time than rerolling to share a spawn.

## The 10–20 band is where the launch is won

This is the first stretch with real choice, and the first place a bad plan shows up.

**Alliance:** Westfall is the default. It feeds the Deadmines (roughly 17–26) and has dense quest loops around Sentinel Hill. If Westfall is a warzone, Loch Modan and Darkshore are quieter alternatives with decent density.

**Horde:** the Barrens is the default — long, spread out, and crowded. Wailing Caverns (roughly 17–24) sits right there. If the Barrens is unbearable, Silverpine Forest is the calmer lane.

Practical rules for this band:

- **Follow the quest chain, not every quest.** Skip low-density quests; a quest that takes 20 minutes in a congested zone is worse than grinding two levels in a quiet corner.
- **Do the dungeon once for the quest rewards.** Ragefire Chasm and the Deadmines both have quest chains with strong early rewards. One clean run, not five.
- **Do not train every spell.** Gold is tight before 20; buy the spells you actually cast, bank the rest for a weapon at 20.

## Pacing (rough, not gospel)

- **Normal pace, reading quests:** about 15–20 hours to 20.
- **Experienced, skipping text:** about 8–10 hours.
- **Launch week with queues and contested tags:** add a few hours no matter what.

These are estimates from the Classic loop, not measured Forever numbers. Treat them as planning ranges.

## Do not do these on day one

- **Do not vendor every green item** if you have an enchanter in the group — those greens are enchanting materials.
- **Do not fight for a single named mob** in a crowded zone. Come back at off-peak, or move on.
- **Do not level two crafting professions** before 20. One gathering profession is enough until your gold flow stabilizes.

## The one-hour setup that saves the week

Before launch, spend an hour on:

1. Keybinds and action bars you can use without looking.
2. The quest/map addon, configured and tested.
3. A bank alt plan — where your materials go, who holds the gold.

That hour is worth more than any leveling trick. For the 20–40 stretch, the [professions guide](/guides/professions-first-week) covers what to level while you grind, and [dual spec at 40](/guides/dual-spec-at-40) explains when your build opens up.
