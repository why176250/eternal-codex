## The honest short answer

- **Want the easiest solo leveling?** Hunter or Warlock.
- **Want instant groups?** Tank or healer — Warrior, Druid, Priest, Paladin, Shaman.
- **Want raid damage with the least gear competition?** Mage, Warlock, or Hunter.
- **Want to do everything on one character?** Druid.

## The comparison

| Class | Leveling | Group demand | Gear competition | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Slow | Very high (tank) | High | Best if you want to tank; painful solo start. |
| Paladin | Steady | High (healer) | Medium | Safe, durable, and always wanted as a healer. |
| Hunter | Fastest | Medium | Low | The solo player's class. Pet does the work. |
| Rogue | Medium | Medium | High | Strong single-target; thin defenses. |
| Priest | Medium | Very high (healer) | Low for healers | Never wait for a group; Shadow needs hit gear. |
| Shaman | Medium | High (healer) | Medium | Totems make you the group's utility pick. |
| Mage | Medium | Medium | Medium | Portals, food, AoE farming; contested farm spots. |
| Warlock | Fast | Medium | Low | Sustain and free mount at 40. |
| Druid | Medium | High (any role) | High (feral/caster split) | The most flexible class in the game. |

## Pick by how you actually play

**You play solo most of the time.** Hunter, Warlock, or Mage. All three level without depending on groups; Hunter is the fastest, Warlock the most forgiving, Mage the best farmer.

**You want to be the person groups need.** Warrior (tank) or Priest (healer). You will never wait for a dungeon, and you will be blamed for every bad pull — that is the deal.

**You want flexibility.** Druid. Bear tank, cat DPS, or healer on one character. The cost is gear competition and a messy bank.

**You want raid damage without loot drama.** Mage, Warlock, or Hunter. Caster and hunter gear is less contested than plate and leather melee gear.

**You want PvP.** Rogue or Mage for control; Warrior with a pocket healer; Shaman for burst. Every class can PvP, but the skill ceiling differs.

## What we would pick for launch week

1. **Priest or Paladin** if you want groups and a calm leveling path.
2. **Hunter** if you want to reach 60 fast and farm gold.
3. **Warrior** if you want to tank and do not mind a slow start.
4. **Druid** if you cannot decide and want to keep every door open.

## The one mistake to avoid

Do not pick a class for its raid performance before you have leveled it to 40. Leveling is 80% of your first two weeks, and a class you hate at level 20 does not become fun at 60.
